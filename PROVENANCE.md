# Provenance

Justichuu supplied the direction and custom-license proposal. OpenAI Codex
implemented this release. The surrounding conversation named Claude, Copilot
and Ollama with a huihui_ai/qwen3.5-abliterated model. Their specific contribution
to this new module is not verified; the record states that distinction.
Unknown contributors and influences have an explicit open entry. These names
are credits and context, not signatures or endorsements from those services.

The default contributor record is readable in `u.mjs`. Supply a corrected or
extended record to `createU({contributors: [...]})`; its fingerprint changes.
Such local changes are not uploaded. A published correction belongs in a
reviewable commit. Git history, version tags, release checksums and software
receipts supply different evidence; none alone proves all claims forever.

The visible burn belongs to the UI. Canonical provenance remains in the source
and is returned by `provenance()`. A rotated display alias is still linkable
through public records. Do not rely on the burn for anonymity or erasure.
